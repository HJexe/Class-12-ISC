## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A calm, direct study index that puts the next subject and resource in view. Refuse the hero-first neon treatment and make the useful content the visual center.

**OWN-WORLD:** Dark-only interface: deep green-black ground (`#111a17`), raised evergreen surfaces, soft ivory text, muted sage structure, and one warm-gold selection mark. Borrow the timetable's precise row alignment and terse hierarchy; the site remains a standard web resource hub, never a timetable, glass rack, or control panel. Keep the existing logo and product copy.

**STORY:** Students recognize the three streams, find a subject or topic, scan its folders, open a note, then use the existing previous/next, Drive, download, share, reload, and fullscreen controls. Recent updates, routes, keyboard shortcuts, theme accents, and mobile pane switching remain available.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Keep a compact top bar with the current name, Home, Directory, Disclaimer, Updates, and Go to Notes. On Home, retain the current title, supporting copy, actions, stats, and stream content in a tighter left-aligned layout. In Directory, keep stream and subject controls plus search in the left rail; align folder and note rows in measured bands beside the document preview and its actions. A single gold marker identifies the current stream or subject. At phone width, preserve the existing Directory / Document Preview switch and stack the controls in reading order.
